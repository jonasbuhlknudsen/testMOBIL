<product_requirements>
The Nerdværket app is an Expo mobile application designed to control an iDot-3 LED Pixel Screen via BLE, aiming for full feature parity and superior functionality compared to the LOY PLAY app. The core problem is to provide robust BLE connectivity (FEE7, UART, FFF0 profiles, MTU 247, auto-reconnect, GATT queue, JSON command schema) and comprehensive screen controls (Solid Color, Animations, Image Transfer, Games, Text Scroller, Power, Brightness). The UI/UX should be LOY PLAY-inspired with tab-based navigation (Connect, Visuals, Media, Games, Settings), a dark theme, gradients, blur-cards, clear typography, large touch targets, and Ionicons.

Implementation so far includes:
- Robust BLE architecture (multi-profile, global GATT queue, protocol engine).
- Functional color controls (HSV, CCT, RGB/W/CCT), gamma, brightness/speed sliders, power controls.
- Advanced features: DIY Editor, Music/Mic modes, IC/Pixel setup, Timers & Schedules, Groups & Sync.
- Extensive LOY PLAY feature parity: Graffiti & Draw Mode, Music Visualizer (8 types, 5 color modes), Voice Commands, Car Mode, Gaming Hub (12+ games, custom builder), Smart Features (notifications, weather, QR codes, social media, fitness), Timer & Scheduler (4 clock types, scheduled programs), Device Groups (multi-device sync).
- Enhanced UI/UX with Reanimated transitions, refined typography, and improved component styling.
- Frontend auto-navigation to bypass initial logo screen issues.
</product_requirements>
